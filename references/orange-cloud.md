# Orange Cloud Reference

## What was studied

The reference was used for:

- Warm daylight environmental layers.
- Restrained glass-like islands.
- Clear spatial separation.
- Platform-specific composition.
- Orange as a focused brand/action accent.

## What was not copied

- Routes.
- Business modules.
- Product data model.
- Authentication flow.
- Original source files.
- Original icons, screenshots, or branding assets.

## Transfer rule

Borrow visual principles and interaction quality. Rebuild independently for the target product's real information architecture.
